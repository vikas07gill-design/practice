Node.js DMS Integration (existing)

Since your DMS is already developed in Node, TaxChinta can consume it directly so you avoid rebuilding storage.

  • POST /api/documents/upload for upload from workflow screens
  • GET /api/documents/:id for instant retrieval in review queues
  • GET /api/documents?clientId=... for client-level history
  • Optional webhook for processing status + OCR indexing
